Master Workshops & Diplomas

Curriculum Philosophy

Choose Your Creative Discipline

At Sugar Bloom Academy, we divide our master curriculum into four rigorous artistic disciplines. Each workshop is structured around small 8-student cohorts to ensure individual workstation guidance.

Whether you are taking your first steps in buttercream palette painting or engineering 5-tier grand wedding cakes, our master instructors teach the underlying structural science and aesthetic principles behind every swirl.

Structured Growth

The Sugarcraft Skill Path

Our curriculum is designed as a continuous progression path. Advance your skills step-by-step from foundational buttercream control to master diploma execution.

Level 1 2 Days • Foundations

Beginner Foundations

Learn sponge levelling, Swiss buttercream preparation, basic palette knife pressure, and foundational piping tip mechanics. No prior baking experience required.

Level 2 3 Days • Precision

Intermediate Artistry

Master 90-degree sharp-edge ganache scraping, seamless fondant wrapping, hand-piped wired gumpaste flowers, and watercolor gradient blending.

Level 3 4 Days • Architecture

Advanced Sugarcraft

Construct multi-tier doweling systems, steam translucent wafer paper sails, gild 24K gold foil, and execute intricate Lambeth royal icing drop-strings.

Master Diploma 2 Weeks • Professional

Professional Diploma Certification

Complete grand 5-tier wedding architecture, design client tasting boxes, establish studio pricing economics, and photograph your master portfolio.

Pillar 01 • Fondant Precision

Ganache Geometry & 90° Edge Control

Acrylic scraper leveling techniques that yield flawless 90-degree corners under fondant. Learn precise temperature conditioning for Agostoni dark chocolate couverture to create rock-solid structural undercoats.

✓ Acrylic plate top and bottom leveling protocol
✓ 2:1 dark chocolate to cream ratio stability for warm transport
✓ Elimination of corner bulges and fondant pinholes
