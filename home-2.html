Level 2 & 3 Course

Fondant Edge & Texture Perfection

Learn the science of marshmallow and gel-based fondants. Master sharp 90-degree edges over acrylic ganache plates, marble color blending, and embossed lace textures.

  • ✓ Acrylic plate ganache smoothing technique
  • ✓ Seamless fondant wrapping without tearing or elephant skin
  • ✓ Metallic bronze and 24k gold leaf gilding application

Level 1 & 2 Course

Sculptural Buttercream Palette

Transform Swiss meringue buttercream into oil-painting style floral canvases. Discover palette knife pressure control, color mixing theory, and silky smoothing secrets.

  • ✓ Swiss & Italian meringue temperature stabilization
  • ✓ Palette knife stroke dynamics for petals and foliage
  • ✓ Watercolor gradient blending on cake sides
View Buttercream Workshop
Level 2 & 3 Course

Botanical Floral Piping & Gumpaste

Hand-pipe lifelike English Roses, Peonies, Ranunculus, and Dahlias directly onto cake tiers or on flower nails for wired bouquet arrangements.

  • ✓ Petal tip angle and wrist rotation mechanics
  • ✓ Edible petal dust shading for botanical realism
  • ✓ Wire stems and floral tape bouquet assembly
View Piping Workshop
Master Diploma Course

Tiered Wedding Cake Architecture

Learn structural engineering for 3 to 5-tier wedding cakes. Master hidden dowels, central locking rods, transport box design, and venue assembly protocols.

  • ✓ Heavy-tier internal support & wooden dowel systems
  • ✓ Floating separator tiers & acrylic spacers
  • ✓ Client consultation pricing and tasting box marketing
