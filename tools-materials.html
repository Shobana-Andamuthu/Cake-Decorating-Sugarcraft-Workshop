Professional Setup

Inside Your Workshop Kit

When you arrive at Sugar Bloom Academy, your personal workstation is fully pre-set with commercial-grade, artisan equipment. We eliminate the frustration of flimsy plastic utensils so you can focus entirely on executing razor-sharp ganache geometry, impasto palette knife flowers, and delicate sugar flora.

Every tool in your kit has been rigorously selected and custom-tested by Chef Vivienne LaRue. From weighted turntables to surgical-grade scalpel blades, experience the effortless precision that true master-level tools provide.

Student Preparation

What You Need to Bring & Expect

At Sugar Bloom Academy, we elevate learning fine sugarcraft into a seamless, luxury atelier experience. Every workstation comes fully pre-set with sterilised commercial equipment, imported European ingredients, chef linen aprons, and master specification workbooks. You only need to bring a few personal essentials to maximize your practical studio sessions.

✓

Comfortable Studio Footwear

Closed-toe non-slip shoes or supportive sneakers designed for standing comfortably at marble workstations during 6-hour hands-on sessions.

✓

Personal Notebook or Tablet

To jot down custom gel tinting formulas, temperature charts, icing consistency adjustments, and personalized instructor feedback during live demos.

✓

Smartphone or Studio Camera

For step-by-step video capturing during complex techniques and studio portfolio photos at our end-of-class lightbox photo station.

✓

Cake Transport Container Box

A tall cake carrier or sturdy cardboard box (min 10" x 10" x 14") with anti-slip base mats to safely transport your finished multi-tiered creation home.

✓

Hair Tie & Studio Attire

Hair ties or bands for long hair. We provide embroidered studio linen aprons to protect your clothing, but recommend short sleeves or fitted sleeves.

✓

Passion for Fine Sugarcraft

An enthusiastic focus on refining wrist mechanics, absorbing structural physics, and joining an intimate group of 8 dedicated cake artists.
