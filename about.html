Our Heritage

Where Cake Becomes Fine Art

"We believe a cake is not merely dessert—it is a temporary architectural monument built to celebrate life's grandest milestones."

Founded by Chef Vivienne LaRue following her graduation from Le Cordon Bleu Paris and years spent apprenticing under French master patissiers, Sugar Bloom Academy was created to bridge the gap between traditional bakery baking and contemporary fine art sculpture.

We reject mass-production shortcuts, artificial bucket frostings, and rushed timelines. Instead, we teach artists how to slow down, master 0.1mm ganache scrapers, wire botanical petals with realistic botanical precision, and engineer multi-tier wedding structures that command awe at luxury wedding receptions.

Master Faculty

Meet Your Master Instructors

Learn directly from world-renowned pastry chefs, structural engineers, and botanical sugar artists.

Founder & Master Instructor

Chef Vivienne LaRue

Le Cordon Bleu Paris • 14 Years Master Craftsmanship

Specializing in grand tiered wedding cake architecture and sharp-edge ganache geometry, Chef Vivienne has trained over 1,200 cake artists worldwide and contributed to major global pastry publications. Her signature 90-degree acrylic levelling method revolutionized modern cake decorating standards.

Before founding Sugar Bloom Academy, Vivienne served as Senior Executive Pastry Chef at Maison de la Sugarcraft in Paris and won numerous international awards for tiered cake structural innovation.

Specialties: Tier Architecture • Ganache Geometry • 24K Gold Gilding
Botanical Sugar Specialist

Elena Rostova

Royal Horticultural Society Exhibitor • 10 Years Experience

Elena brings botanical realism to sugar flower sculpting. Her wired sugar peonies, garden roses, orchids, and dahlias are renowned for being visually indistinguishable from fresh garden blooms.

She guides students through paper-thin gumpaste rolling, European veining mold impressions, wire stem taping, and multi-layered dry dust color shading techniques.

Specialties: Gumpaste Peonies • Stem Wiring • Petal Dust Shading
Structural & Ganache Architect

Marcus Vance

Pastry Arts Master • Structural Engineer

Marcus focuses on the structural mechanics of multi-tier wedding cakes—teaching students internal wooden dowel grids, stainless steel central locking rods, and venue transport box safety.

His engineering formulas ensure that 5-tier monolith cakes can be safely transported over long distances and withstand warm outdoor summer reception temperatures without tilting or collapsing.

Specialties: Dowel Grids • Wafer Paper Sails • Venue Transport
