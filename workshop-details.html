Master Curriculum

Curriculum Architecture & Modules

Every participant builds a full 3-tier dummy wedding cake to take home, along with a portfolio photo setup and master certification.

Module 01

Acrylic Plate Ganache Geometry & 90° Edges

Master top and bottom acrylic plates to achieve pin-sharp edges over dark chocolate ganache undercoats.

Module 02

Heavy Tier Internal Dowel Grids & Locking Rods

Weight-bearing engineering, wooden dowel grid placements, and central threaded stainless steel locking rods.

Module 03

Botanical Wired Sugar Flora & Dust Realism

Hand-sculpting gumpaste peonies, English garden roses, foliage wiring, and edible dust shading.

Module 04

Wafer Paper Sails & 24K Gold Leaf Gilding

Conditioning rice wafer paper into translucent organic sails and gilding 24K gold foil onto cake sides.

Module 05

Client Tasting Boxes, Pricing & Transport Protocols

Costing calculation models, high-end wedding quote presentation, tasting box packaging, and venue setup.

Diploma Included

Certified Master Sugarcraft Artist Certificate awarded upon course completion.

5-Stage Methodology

Visual Learning Journey

From raw ingredients to grand venue delivery, experience our step-by-step master progression.

01

Prepare

Acrylic plate setup, crumb coating levelling, and color palette blending.

02

Shape

Fondant draping, razor edge scraping, and seamless corner smoothing.

03

Decorate

Sculpting gumpaste peonies, English garden roses, and foliage wiring.

04

Refine

Applying 24K gold foil, steaming wafer paper sails, and dust shading.

05

Present

Tier stacking, central locking rod installation, and venue transport.
